Who We Are

A Family Bakery Built on Love

Sweet Bliss was born in a small home kitchen in 2010, when founder Anjali started baking cakes for friends and neighbours. Word spread, orders grew, and in 2013 we opened our first shop.

Today we serve hundreds of families with the same care and quality — no preservatives, no shortcuts, just pure handcrafted goodness. Every cake tells a story, and we can't wait to tell yours.

Our Journey

From Home Kitchen to City Favourite

2010

A Sweet Beginning

Anjali starts baking custom birthday cakes from her home kitchen in Pune, taking orders via word-of-mouth.

2013

First Shop Opens

Demand outgrows the home kitchen. Sweet Bliss Bakery opens its first retail store in Koregaon Park.

2017

500+ Happy Customers

We hit our 500th customer milestone and launch online ordering via WhatsApp for easy, seamless orders.

2021

Custom Cake Studio

We open a dedicated custom cake design studio offering wedding cakes, tier cakes and sculpted designs.

Now

Still Growing, Still Baking

80+ cake varieties, same-day delivery across the city, and still every cake made fresh with love — just like day one.
